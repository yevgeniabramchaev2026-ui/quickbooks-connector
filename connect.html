Connect or reconnect

The BELISSE MOZHDE WIG MAKING L.L.C. QuickBooks Connector is a private integration. Only the owner of BELISSE MOZHDE WIG MAKING L.L.C. connects it, and only to the company's own QuickBooks Online account.

How the connection is made

  1. The owner signs in to their Intuit account through Intuit's standard authorization page.
  2. The owner selects the BELISSE MOZHDE WIG MAKING L.L.C. QuickBooks Online company and approves read access to accounting data (scope com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting).
  3. The resulting authorization is stored only on the owner's computer.

Reconnect

If the connection expires or is revoked, the owner repeats the same steps. No other business can connect to this integration.
