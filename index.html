BELISSE MOZHDE WIG MAKING L.L.C. QuickBooks Connector

Private · Read-only

This is a private, internal integration operated by BELISSE MOZHDE WIG MAKING L.L.C. for its own use. It connects one QuickBooks Online company owned by BELISSE MOZHDE WIG MAKING L.L.C. to a locally running assistant tool so that the owner can review the company's own accounting data and reports.

What it does

  • Reads accounting data and reports (for example company information, profit and loss, balance sheet) from the owner's own QuickBooks Online company.
  • Operates in read-only mode: creating, updating and deleting data is disabled.
  • Is not offered to the public, is not sold, and does not accept connections from other businesses.
