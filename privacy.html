Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how the BELISSE MOZHDE WIG MAKING L.L.C. QuickBooks Connector (the "Connector"), operated by BELISSE MOZHDE WIG MAKING L.L.C. for its own internal use, handles data from the connected QuickBooks Online company. Contact: yevgeniabramchaev2026@gmail.com.

2. Data we access

With the owner's authorization (scope com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting), the Connector can read accounting data of the connected company, such as company information, customers, vendors, invoices, bills, accounts, transactions and financial reports. It does not access payment card or bank login credentials. It operates in read-only mode.

3. How data is used

Data is retrieved only when the owner makes a request, and is used only to answer that request (for example, to summarize a report). We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with other businesses.

4. Where data is processed

  • The Connector runs locally on the owner's computer. Authorization tokens are stored only on that computer.
  • When the owner asks the assistant application (Claude, provided by Anthropic) a question, the relevant data retrieved from QuickBooks is sent to that assistant service to generate the answer, and is handled under the assistant provider's own terms and privacy policy.
  • QuickBooks Online data itself remains hosted by Intuit.

5. Storage and retention

The Connector does not maintain its own database of QuickBooks data. Authorization tokens are kept on the owner's computer until the Connector is disconnected, after which they are deleted.

6. Security

Access uses Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization. Credentials and tokens are kept in local files that are not published or shared. Write, update and delete operations are disabled.

7. Your choices

The connection can be revoked at any time in QuickBooks Online. See Disconnect.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date shows the current version. Questions: yevgeniabramchaev2026@gmail.com.
